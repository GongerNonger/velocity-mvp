## 1. Current institutional numbers

### Enrollment
- **Fall 2025 total enrollment: 48,670 students** — a record high and 3.98% increase over Fall 2024. Source: [UVU News, "UVU Continues Educating the Most Utahns With New Record Enrollment"](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2025/fall-2025-enrollment.html)
- **Fall 2024 total enrollment: 46,809 students** — at the time, also a record. Source: [UVU News, Oct 2024](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/10/uvu-enrollment-for-fall-2024.html)
- UVU is the **largest public university in Utah by enrollment**. Source: [About UVU](https://www.uvu.edu/about/)

### Student demographics
- **First-generation students: ~41% of the student body** (as of Fall 2024 — the most recent published figure; first-gen enrollment surged 18.54% year-over-year). Source: [UVU News, Oct 2024](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/10/uvu-enrollment-for-fall-2024.html)
- **Pell-eligible percentage:** TBD — verify with UVU IR before publishing. (Not surfaced in public 2025 press; the Common Data Set 2023-24 at https://www.uvu.edu/birs/docs/info_about_uvu/common_data_set/cds2023-24.pdf likely contains it.)

### Programs and structure
- **300+ degrees and credentials offered**, spanning certificates, associate degrees, bachelor's degrees, and master's degrees. Source: [UVU Academic Catalog](https://catalog.uvu.edu/programs/)
- **Seven (7) colleges and schools** (per the 2024 commencement announcement: "Convocations for the institution's seven colleges and schools"). Source: [UVU News, May 2024 commencement](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/04/2024_04_29_commencement.html). See section 7 below for the full list.
- **Dual-mission model**: UVU is one of the few institutions in the country combining a four-year teaching university with open-admissions community-college access. Source: [Dual-Mission Model, UVU Magazine](https://www.uvu.edu/news/magazine/2018/spring2018_dualmission.html) and [Inside Higher Ed coverage](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/2018/01/16/utah-valley-university-thrives-being-both-community-college-and-university)

### Completion and outcomes
- **6-year completion rate: ~41%** for the 2023-2024 cohort (1,053 of 2,585 completed within 150% of normal time). Source aggregator: [College Tuition Compare](https://www.collegetuitioncompare.com/edu/230737/utah-valley-university/graduation/). Cross-check with [UVU Outcomes](https://www.uvu.edu/birs//info/outcomes.html).
- **8-year completion rate: 46% (May 2024)** — UVU exceeded its 45% goal a year early. Source: [UVU News, Feb 2024](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/02/2024_02_14_graduation.html)
- **One-year retention rate: 70%** — reached two years ahead of the original goal. Source: [UVU 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.uvu.edu/annual-report/exceptional-results.html)
- **73.4% of UVU graduates secure high-demand, high-wage jobs.** Source: [UVU 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.uvu.edu/annual-report/exceptional-results.html)
- **Carnegie "Opportunity College and University — Higher Access, Higher Earnings" designation** (April 2025); awarded to only ~16% of U.S. institutions. Source: [UVU 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.uvu.edu/annual-report/exceptional-results.html)

### Degrees awarded
- **Class of 2025: 12,940 graduates earning 17,278 degrees and certificates** — the largest class in UVU history. Source: [UVU News, "UVU Celebrates the Largest Graduating Class in School History"](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2025/uvu-celebrates-largest-graduating-class-commencement-2025.html)
- About one-third of 2025 graduates were first-generation; ~84% from Utah; ages 16-71; 47 states and 53 countries represented. Same source.
- **Class of 2024: 10,197 graduates / 12,499 degrees and certificates**, including 3,406 first-generation students. Source: [UVU News, April 2024](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/04/2024_04_29_commencement.html)
- Bachelor's / associate / certificate breakdown by year: **TBD — verify with UVU IR.** The annual report aggregates "degrees and certificates"; the granular split lives in the IR fact book.

### Advising
- Advising is organized across **eight units** — seven college-based advisement teams plus the First-Year Advising Center, all reporting through a Sr. Director of Academic Advising. Source: [UVU Office of University Advising — About](https://www.uvu.edu/advising/university/about.html)
- **Advisor:student ratio: TBD — verify with UVU IR.** Not published on UVU.edu in any page I could surface. Anecdotal industry benchmark is ~300:1 (already used in landing-page copy), but UVU's actual figure should be confirmed before publishing as a UVU-specific stat.

## 2. Strategic plan: Vision 2030

Source: [UVU Vision 2030](https://www.uvu.edu/vision2030/)

The plan is organized around **three pillars** — Include, Engage, Achieve.

1. **Include** — "Provide accessible, flexible, and affordable education in an environment that is inclusive for all." [Include pillar page](https://www.uvu.edu/vision2030/include.html)
2. **Engage** — "Strengthen student learning and societal impact through collaboration with community and industry." [Engage pillar page](https://www.uvu.edu/vision2030/engage.html)
3. **Achieve** — "Enhance student success through improved recruitment, retention, and completion." [Achieve pillar page](https://www.uvu.edu/vision2030/achieve.html)

### Quantitative target
- **Achieve goal: 50% institutional completion rate by 2030.** Source: [Vision 2030 Achieve page](https://www.uvu.edu/vision2030/achieve.html)

## 6. Existing UVU advising / student-success / AI tools

These already exist on campus. Velocity's positioning should be **complementary**, not "we're inventing this from scratch."

### Ask Wilson (AI assistant)
- UVU's in-house AI chatbot, built by CIO Christina Baum's team with support from the Kahlert Applied AI Institute. Trained on course content, accessible within Canvas, can coach students, generate study guides, and link directly to specific Kaltura lecture timestamps.
- **Scope as of early 2026: 44 courses, ~2,000 students.** Pilot began Fall 2023 with one biology course.
- URLs: [Ask Wilson project page](https://www.uvu.edu/digitaltransformation/project_planning/projects/askwilson.html), [Chatbot FAQ](https://www.uvu.edu/askwilson/chatbot_friendly_faq_page.html), [Roadmap](https://www.uvu.edu/askwilson/roadmap.html)
- News profile: [EdTech Magazine Q&A, Feb 2026](https://edtechmagazine.com/higher/article/2026/02/qa-how-utah-valley-university-using-ai-help-students-succeed)
- **Implication for Velocity**: Ask Wilson is course-scoped (Canvas, in-class help). Velocity is institutional/advising-scoped (degree planning, retention, career match). Frame Velocity as the layer above Ask Wilson, not a replacement.

### Wolverine Track (DegreeWorks)
- UVU's degree-audit + planning system. Includes audits, "what-if" analyses, look-ahead, semester schedule planning, and graduation applications. Free; integrated with myUVU.
- URLs: [Wolverine Track](https://www.uvu.edu/wolverinetrack/), [UVU Review profile](https://www.uvureview.com/news/wolverine-track-helps-students-stay-on-track/)
- **Implication for Velocity**: Wolverine Track is a **degree audit** (what requirements remain). Velocity's "personalized 4-year plan generated at enrollment" is a different artifact — sequencing, prerequisite optimization, career alignment. Position as upstream of Wolverine Track.

### Civitas Learning partnership
- UVU uses Civitas's Student Impact Platform for predictive retention analytics, faculty alerts, and re-enrollment workflows.
- **Documented outcomes** (Civitas case study): +3 pts retention, eliminated 100+ registration holds, re-enrolled 600+ students with 40+ credits, faculty alerts resolved within one business day.
- UVU also runs **Student Success Specialists** (near-peer outreach) wired into the Civitas platform.
- Sources: [Civitas case study — "From Connection to Completion"](https://www.civitaslearning.com/customer-success-stories/from-connection-to-completion-how-uvus-human-centered-approach-boosts-retention-at-scale/), [UVU Civitas page](https://www.uvu.edu/biservices/civitas.html), [UVU KB article](https://kb.uvu.edu/servicecenter/civitas-learning), [Civitas podcast on UVU](https://www.civitaslearning.com/podcast/how-uvus-student-success-specialists-build-connection-through-proactive-outreach/)
- **Implication for Velocity**: Civitas already owns "predictive risk model + workflow tool for staff." Velocity's edge is **student-facing personalized plans + career match**, plus a higher-resolution AI advisor experience. Don't pitch against Civitas; pitch alongside it.

### Kahlert Applied AI Institute
- Launched November 2024. Mission: AI faculty development, student academic support, community impact / workforce readiness.
- Led by Rachael Hutchings, J.D. (Executive Director) and Barclay Burns, Ph.D. (Chief AI Innovation Officer).
- URLs: [Institute](https://www.uvu.edu/ai-institute/), [Announcement](https://www.uvu.edu/news/2024/11/applied-artificial-intelligence-institute-announcement.html)
- **Implication for Velocity**: The Institute is the obvious campus champion / co-pilot for a Velocity rollout. Name-drop it.
