# Velocity — AI Academic Advisor

**One-page leave-behind / deal sheet · May 2026**

---

## More students, fewer barriers, faster degrees.

Velocity is an AI academic advisor that helps universities lift completion rates and give advisors their week back.

---

## The problem

The national median academic advisor carries **296 students**; at large public universities the load runs higher.[^1] Routine work — prerequisite-checking, degree-audit reading, "can I take this next?" — crowds out the human conversations that actually move retention. Meanwhile, every dollar in Utah's public higher-ed system is being reallocated under HB 265 toward workforce-aligned, timely-completion outcomes.[^2] Advising is the leverage point. Hiring more advisors at scale isn't realistic.

## The solution

Velocity sits beside the advisor, not in front of the student alone. It ingests a course catalog and a student record (CSV is enough — no SIS integration required to start), and within minutes produces a personalized degree path, flags off-track students, and answers the routine prerequisite and scheduling questions that consume most advisor hours.

## Three key features

1. **Personalized pathway engine.** Every student sees the fastest credible route to graduation given their completed courses, declared major, and Utah workforce demand signals.
2. **Advisor caseload dashboard.** One screen ranks every advisee by risk, flags missing prerequisites, and pre-writes the meeting prep so 30-minute appointments start with insight, not data entry.
3. **AI chat trained on the institution's catalog.** Students get instant answers at 11 p.m. — when most "should I drop this class?" decisions actually get made — with full audit trail visible to advisors.

## Proof points

- **UVU hit its 2025 completion goal a year early** (46% in May 2024) and is now targeting 50% by 2030.[^3][^4] Advising capacity is the constraint.
- **Adjacent benchmarks** show AI copilots saving 10+ hours per advisor per week, with one firm logging 700 saved hours in a single 28-day case study.[^5][^6]
- **Advisor retention research** (Tyton Partners via NACADA) shows advisor 5-year retention rises above 50% only when caseloads dip below 300 — Velocity makes existing advisors functionally equivalent to a larger team.[^1]
- **Built for Utah.** Pathway recommendations weight Utah Department of Workforce Services demand data, directly answering HB 265's workforce-alignment requirement.[^2]

## The pilot ask

**30 days. Zero dollars. Zero integration risk.**

- Scope: ~300 students, one college (recommended: UVU College of Engineering and Technology, ~5,200 students across 7 departments).[^7]
- We deliver: branded UVU instance, student-facing demo, advisor admin view, weekly metrics, Day-30 readout.
- UVU provides: one executive sponsor, anonymized CSV data (or we'll use simulated), five advisors for feedback.
- Success metrics (public, falsifiable): student activation ≥ 60%, recommendation engagement ≥ 45%, advisor NPS ≥ +30, ≥ 3 hours saved per advisor per week, ≥ 10 newly-identified off-track students.
- If we hit 4 of 5, UVU converts to a 12-month contract: **$100K platform + $25/student/year** (~$1.1M ARR at 41,000 students). If we miss, we walk.

## Why now

UVU is the inflection institution: record 46,809-student enrollment, 41% first-generation, president publicly committed to digital transformation, and a legislature actively rewarding measurable workforce alignment.[^4][^8] The institution that wins a public, defensible AI-advising rollout in 2026 sets the template for the rest of USHE and the country.
