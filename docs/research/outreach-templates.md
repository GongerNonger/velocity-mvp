## Sequencing notes (internal, not part of templates)

1. Send warm-intro requests (c) first. Warm beats cold ~10x in higher ed.
2. If no warm path inside 7 days, send (a) to the Provost on a Tuesday morning, Mountain Time.
3. Send (b) to the CIO 48 hours after (a). Mentioning a parallel Provost conversation creates internal urgency.
4. Do not pitch Vision 2030, HB 265, and digital transformation in the same email — pick the one most relevant to the recipient.
5. Track replies by hook used; iterate the hook before iterating the body.
